# NHS Pay Calculator by Band — Agenda for Change Take-Home

> NHS Agenda for Change pay is banded, and the take-home figure matters more than the headline because the NHS pension is a large deduction.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

Bands 2 to 8a cover most NHS roles, each with entry and top points reached through years of service.

The NHS pension is a **defined benefit** scheme with tiered contributions rising with pay, which is a much bigger deduction than a typical auto-enrolment pension but buys a guaranteed income rather than a pot.

That combination means NHS take-home is lower than a private-sector salary of the same headline figure, while the total package is often better. Comparing headline salaries alone gets this backwards.

High-cost area supplements apply in and around London and change the figures again.

## What this does not cover

It does not model unsocial hours payments under Section 2, on-call, or overtime, which for many shift-working staff are a large share of actual pay.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
